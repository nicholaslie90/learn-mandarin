# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Adult self-learners studying Mandarin, working through HSK 1–9 vocabulary. Self-directed, session-based study (flashcards, quiz, stroke writing, pronunciation, reading). Motivation is intrinsic; streaks, points, and ranks exist to support habit formation, not to entertain children.

## Product Purpose

All-in-one HSK vocabulary mastery tool: spaced-repetition flashcards, handwriting canvas, pronunciation lab, reading comprehension, dictionary, and grammar reference. Success looks like a consistent daily study habit and measurable vocabulary mastery through SRS retention.

## Brand Personality

Playful but refined — "Duolingo for adults". Encouraging, warm, occasionally witty; never cartoonish or childish. A knowledgeable lǎoshī (老师) with a sense of humor. Cultural texture (chengyu, seal stamps, festival motifs) is the source of charm.

## Anti-references

- Childish edtech: cartoon mascots, candy-primary palettes, patronizing copy
- Corporate LMS stiffness: "Performance updates are successfully synced to your dashboard"
- Generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics; decorative glassmorphism without purpose

## Design Principles

- Celebrate progress, don't just display it — streaks, ranks, and badges should feel earned. Delight lands at moments (completion, milestones, rank-ups), not on pages.
- The study flow is sacred: delight never delays, blocks, or distracts from learning tasks.
- Personality lives in copy and moments; components and layout stay consistent and familiar.
- Motion conveys state and feedback; every animation ships with a reduced-motion alternative.
- Chinese cultural motifs (seal red, chengyu, festival themes) beat generic confetti clichés.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for body text; `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation; aria semantics preserved as UI evolves; playful copy must never obscure meaning or instructions.
